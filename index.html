About Our Foundation

Citizen Welfare Foundation is built on the core pillars of social justice, active empathy, and transparent welfare service. Our goal is to empower underprivileged communities and create equal developmental opportunities for all.

Frequently Asked Questions

How can I join Citizen Welfare Foundation as a volunteer?

You can click on the "JOIN OUR MISSION" button on the website and fill out the volunteer onboarding form. Our team will contact you shortly.

Where does the foundation operate?

We actively operate through grassroots drives, community welfare centers, and emergency relief programs across local regions.

How can I contribute or support your initiatives?

You can support us by volunteering your time, sharing our campaigns, or reaching out to us directly through WhatsApp or Email.
