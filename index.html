About Our Foundation

Citizen Welfare Foundation samajik nyay, sakriy sahayata aur nishpaksh sewa bhav par aadharit ek samajik pehal hai. Hamara lakshya zarooratmand nagrikon tak sidhi sahayata aur kanooni jagrukta pahunchana hai.

🤝 Ground Integrity

Direct on-field humanitarian aid bina kisi madhyastha ya formality ke.

🎯 Immediate Action

Grassroots level par jahan sahayata ki sabse zyaada tatkalik aavashyakta hoti hai.

🌱 Youth & Social Change

Youth leadership ke madhyam se shiksha, samman aur adhikar jagrukta ka prasar.

How can I join Citizen Welfare Foundation as a volunteer?

Aap upar diye gaye "JOIN OUR MISSION" button par click karke registration form fill kar sakte hain ya WhatsApp par direct sampark kar sakte hain.

Is there any joining or membership fee?

Nahi, foundation ke social drives, relief distribution aur voluntary campaigns se judne ke liye koi fee nahi hai.

What areas do you work in?

Hum mukhya roop se shiksha sahayata, citizen rights awareness, animal welfare aur zarooratmand parivaron tak tatkalik relief drives conduct karte hain.
