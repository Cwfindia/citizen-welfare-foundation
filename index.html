Who We Are

About Our Foundation

Citizen Welfare Foundation — Creating Change. Inspiring Humanity.

Citizen Welfare Foundation is a social welfare organization founded on 14 March 2025 by Anurag Choudhary in Kanpur Nagar, Uttar Pradesh.

We work to support people in need and create a stronger, more compassionate community through meaningful welfare initiatives, community support, and direct assistance.

Our philosophy goes beyond simply helping someone. We believe that one act of kindness can inspire many more. Through our work, we aim to build a lasting chain of humanity where people help one another and carry kindness forward.

Our Vision: A society where helping others is not an obligation, but a shared responsibility.

Our Promise: We are committed to serving with sincerity, dignity, transparency, and compassion—because every helping hand has the power to create a difference.

📍 Based in: Kanpur Nagar, Uttar Pradesh | 🗓️ Established: 14 March 2025 | 👤 Founder: Anurag Choudhary
